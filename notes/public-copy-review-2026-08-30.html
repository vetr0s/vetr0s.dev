The colophon should be rewritten before publication. Most of its design description predates the new stylesheet. It also understates Ginger Bill's influence. The rest of the public copy needs three factual corrections and one decision about the About page.

Bottom line on the colophon

Publish blocker The current colophon describes a 60rem centered sheet, inline notes at every width, colored headings, and separate page and sheet tones. None of those claims matches the current stylesheet.

The new version should lead with direct credit. The current visual design owes more to gingerBill.org than to any other single site. That influence is structural. It is not limited to note markup.

What came from Ginger Bill

PartCurrent implementationCredit to state
Page geometry A content-box body uses 87.5% width and 12.5% left padding. The sheet caps at 80rem. The reading column uses 60%. The geometry and its proportions were adapted from gingerBill.org.
Typography ET Book sets the body and headings at a 1.25rem reading size. ET Book was discovered through Ginger Bill's site. Tufte CSS remains the upstream font and typesetting source.
Margin notes Notes float beside the article on wide screens. They collapse behind a tap target below 80rem. The current wide-margin treatment follows Ginger Bill's implementation. Tufte CSS supplied the original sidenote pattern.
Headings Large neutral headings use an h2 rule. H2 and h3 self-links reveal a section sign on hover. The heading register and section-sign pattern came from Ginger Bill's site.
Code and tables Inline code and blocks use bordered surfaces. Tables are enclosed and zebra-striped. The code-box treatment follows Ginger Bill's site. The table treatment belongs to the local stylesheet revision.
Site generator The archived ostat generator was modeled after Ginger Bill's Odin generator. Credit this separately from the CSS. His site influenced both the old build system and the current visual design.

The Modus palettes still belong to Protesilaos Stavrou. The Pandoc and Make pipeline is local. The breadcrumb navigation, project listings, and content structure are also local choices.

Recommended credit passage

The current design owes more to Ginger Bill's website than to any other single source. I adapted its page geometry, ET Book typography, wide margin notes, heading scale, section-sign links, and code treatment. The old static-site generator behind this site was also modeled after the Odin generator that builds gingerBill.org.

I found ET Book and this style of document layout through Ginger Bill's work. ET Book and the original sidenote pattern come from Tufte CSS. The color palettes come from Protesilaos Stavrou's Modus themes. Those sources are distinct, and each deserves direct credit.

This passage should replace the current one-line Ginger Bill bullet. Link both the live site and its source repository.
